Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 25, 2026

Overview

Inphoria is a daily knowledge app designed with your privacy in mind. We believe you should be able to learn something new every day without giving up any personal information in return.

Data We Collect

None. Inphoria does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data whatsoever.

What Stays on Your Device

The following information is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted:

  • Your streak count (consecutive days of app usage)
  • Your favourite entries
  • Your like/dislike votes
  • Your notification preferences
  • Cached content for offline access

This data never leaves your device. We have no servers, no databases, and no way to access it.

Network Requests

Inphoria makes a single network request on launch to fetch the latest daily content from a publicly hosted JSON file. No cookies, tokens, identifiers, or personal information are sent with this request.

Analytics & Tracking

Inphoria does not include any analytics, tracking, advertising, or third-party SDKs. We do not use device fingerprinting, and we do not track your usage in any way.

Third-Party Services

Inphoria does not integrate with any third-party services. Reference links within the app open in an in-app browser — the visited websites may have their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Inphoria does not collect any data from anyone, including children. The app is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change our privacy practices, we will update this page. Given that we collect no data, we don't anticipate any changes.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can reach us at inphoria.contact@gmail.com.
